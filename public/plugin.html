Grace Plan

Prepare project proposals from floor plans and room photographs. Grace helps organize source files, map photos to rooms, describe finishes, plan CCTV and network layouts, check calculations, and assemble proposal materials.

Publisher: SYMBIOTYC
Availability: Ukraine
Purchases: None through this plugin.

What it supports

  • Track uploaded floor plans and photographs and keep uncertain room matches explicit.
  • Develop concept visualizations and preliminary security, network, and equipment proposals when the host provides suitable tools.
  • Prepare structured A2, A3, and A4 proposal materials and review calculations and assumptions.

Limits

Tool availability depends on ChatGPT and the user’s account. A concept render is not a parametric BIM model or verified construction document. Users should check room matches, geometry, units, cable routes, electrical loads, equipment compatibility, prices, and conclusions.

This listing describes the ChatGPT skills package. The Grace AI Planner web application at graceplanner.vercel.app is a separate service.
